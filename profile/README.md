We bring together **websites, lead capture, AI, automation, customer communication, and business operations** into connected systems designed for modern service businesses.

## 🛠️ Technology & Infrastructure

Our enterprise systems leverage modern, scalable technologies across:

* **🤖 AI & LLMs:** OpenAI API, Anthropic Claude, custom fine-tuned agents & prompt pipelines
* **⚡ Automation:** n8n, automated webhooks, background queues, and event dispatchers
* **🌐 Web Applications:** Next.js 15 (App Router), React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
* **🔌 APIs & Integrations:** REST, GraphQL, WhatsApp Business API, CRM & SMS Gateways
* **🗄️ Databases & Storage:** PostgreSQL, Turso DB, SQLite, Redis, Vector Databases
* **💬 CRM & Customer Comms:** Unified inbox architectures, live chat agents, and automated dunning
* **📊 Analytics & Attribution:** Multi-touch conversion tracking and performance telemetry
* **☁️ Cloud Infrastructure:** Vercel, Supabase, Docker, Edge Functions, and AWS
